01 The gap

DEXA measures density. That's one piece of the picture.

Traditional density scans show how much mineral is present — one dimension of strength. They don't capture the structure, texture, and architecture that decide whether a bone holds or breaks.

High bone density doesn't always mean strong bones. REMS® sees what density misses.

02 Four data points, one scan

Bone density. Bone quality. Fracture risk. Body composition.

Every REMS® assessment scans the lumbar vertebrae (L1–L4) and femoral neck — the same sites as DEXA — and returns four distinct measurements no other bone assessment delivers together.

01

Bone Mineral Density

BMD at the lumbar vertebrae and femoral neck, plus T-Score and Z-Score — the standard parameters your physician already reads.

02

Fragility Score

A 0–100 score capturing internal bone structure and microarchitecture, independent of BMD — a measurement DEXA cannot produce.

03

5-Year Fracture Risk

Direct fracture probability for spine and hip over the next five years, calculated from your own density and quality data.

04

Body Composition

Body fat percentage, basal metabolic rate, and visceral fat assessment — all from the same single scan.

03 The technology

How REMS® works.

A probe is placed at your lumbar spine and proximal femur — the same sites a DEXA would measure. The sound wave enters; the echo returns. What it reads gets matched against a peer-reviewed reference database of 10,000+ real bone specimens. Your result tells you where you rank, and which one yours resembles.

04 Where REMS® works best

Built for the people DEXA leaves out.

Because REMS® is radiation-free and reads both density and quality, it is clinically appropriate for populations that have had limited bone assessment options.

Pregnancy & postpartum

The only bone assessment safe during pregnancy. Establishes a baseline before the largest bone draw of a woman's life.

Perimenopause & menopause

Bone loss accelerates sharply through the transition. REMS® catches it earlier than standard screening allows.

GLP-1 medication users

Rapid weight loss is linked to measurable bone density decline. A baseline protects what the scale doesn't show.

Type 2 diabetes

Density alone underestimates fracture risk in diabetic patients. Quality assessment is more accurate for this group.

Cancer therapy patients

Treatment-related bone loss is significant. Radiation-free monitoring suits patients already exposed therapeutically.

Active aging & athletes

Performance-oriented individuals tracking bone health alongside training load, with safe repeated monitoring.
