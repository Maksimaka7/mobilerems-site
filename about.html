Founder's story

Clinical precision. Concierge delivery.

MobileREMS exists because waiting two years for the right bone assessment is two years too long. Here is why.

Image Alexandra Kusherets — founder
I brought REMS® here because you don't have time to wait for the system to catch up.

I'm Alexandra Kusherets. I was not looking for bone health. It found me.

A DEXA flagged something I didn't understand. I waited two years for a REMS® provider to travel into Washington. What I learned changed everything.

I wasn't fragile. I didn't need fear. I needed the full picture. Nobody else should wait two years to see what's inside their own bones.

So I founded MobileREMS. You shouldn't have to wait either.
