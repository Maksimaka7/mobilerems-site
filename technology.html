A New Standard in Bone Health Assessment

Bone assessment has a new standard. It earned that in Europe. It is now here.

REMS, Radiofrequency Echographic Multi-Spectrometry, measures bone mineral density, bone quality, internal microarchitecture, and a Fragility Score (a structural fracture-risk indicator), plus body composition. Radiation-free, with results the same day.

Validated in Europe since 2013. Included in Italy's national osteoporosis guidelines. FDA-cleared in the US.

Systematic adoption of new clinical standards takes years, sometimes decades. Your bones do not have that kind of time. You do not have to wait. Echolight REMS® is available in Western Washington, and it is mobile. We come to you.

Where REMS® Fits

No single scan sees everything.

Understanding your bones is complicated, and the tools that measure them are still catching up. There is no single scan that sees all of it, only a set of readings, each revealing a different part of the picture.

DEXA has been the standard for forty years. It measures one thing: bone mineral density, in two dimensions. That made it the reference every other tool is judged against. Standard, though, does not mean complete.

REMS® is a different kind of tool, and a genuinely new one. It reads bone with radiofrequency instead of radiation, and reads something density alone was never built to show: the structural quality of the bone itself, drawn from your own bone.

Density

How much mineral is present. REMS® matches DEXA here: the forty-year reference.

Structure

How that bone is actually built, its structural quality.

Both from a single session, without radiation.

An engineer assessing a structure does not ask how much material was used. The engineer asks: will it hold. Bone deserves the same question. That is the layer REMS® adds.

02 Why REMS® makes the difference

Benefits and advantages of REMS®.

REMS® combines clinical advantages that no other bone health assessment provides in a single technology.

Radiation-free

A safer alternative for regular bone health monitoring, including patients already undergoing radiation treatment.

Measures more than density

Beyond bone mineral density, REMS® reports microarchitecture and composition — a fuller picture of bone strength than density provides alone.

Microarchitecture analysis

Evaluates trabecular thickness and connectivity, cortical thickness, and porosity of bone.

5-year fracture risk

An estimated probability of fracture over the next five years, calculated from your bone mineral density and Fragility Score.

Accurate for complex patients

Validated across conditions like type 2 diabetes, osteoarthritis, scoliosis, and rheumatoid arthritis, where density alone can mislead.

Real-time imaging

Bone properties visualized and analyzed within minutes, in the room.

Earlier detection

Monitors bone health earlier in life and more often than radiation-based methods allow — safe during pregnancy and pre-menopause.

Pre-surgical assessment

Bone evaluation prior to joint replacement or spine fusion surgery to inform surgical planning.

Mobile delivery

Portable technology used in various care settings. We bring it to your practice, organization, or home.

03 Four data points per scan

Bone density. Bone quality. Fracture risk. Body composition.

Every REMS® assessment scans the lumbar vertebrae (L1–L4) and femoral neck — the same sites as DEXA. From a single, radiation-free scan, REMS® delivers four distinct measurement categories: a broader read than density alone.

01

Bone Mineral Density

BMD measured in g/cm² at the lumbar vertebrae and femoral neck, plus T-Score and Z-Score. The standard parameters for osteoporosis diagnosis your physician already reads.

02

Fragility Score

A 0–100 score capturing internal bone structure and microarchitecture. Independent of BMD. The measurement DEXA cannot produce.

03

5-Year Fracture Risk

Fracture probability for spine and hip in the next five years, calculated from your specific density and bone quality data.

04

Body Composition

Body fat percentage, basal metabolic rate, and visceral fat assessment, all from the same scan.

The Mechanism

How REMS® works, briefly.

REMS® sends a sound wave into your bone and listens to what comes back. The echo carries information about your specific bone tissue: its density and its internal structure. That acoustic signature is unique to your bone. No hospital. No radiation. No referral required.

The full mechanism, how the device filters the signal, isolates load-bearing bone, and compares your bone against a reference library of 10,000+ real bone specimens, is worth understanding if you want to know how REMS® produces what it produces.

The Scan Experience

What happens at a REMS® appointment.

A REMS® scan takes about 30 minutes. You lie down. A handheld probe is placed at the lumbar spine and proximal femur. No radiation, no contrast, no medication adjustment. You receive your results in the same session.
