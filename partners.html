The bone health layer

One scan, four readings the density number alone can't give you.

REMS® (Radiofrequency Echographic Multi Spectrometry) reads the spine and hip in real time and returns a full picture of bone strength, not only how much mineral is present.

01

Bone Density

A BMD and T-score comparable to DXA, the measure clinicians already know and trust.

02

Bone Quality

A quality index reflecting how well the bone is built, beyond how much mineral is there.

03

Trabecular Architecture

A read on the internal microarchitecture that helps explain why some patients fracture at normal density.

04

Fragility Score

A five-year fragility estimate that supports earlier, more confident decisions.

Validation summaries, operator certification, and device clearance documentation are available on request while our published references are being finalized.

Your patients

The patients already in your schedule who benefit most.

REMS® suits people who are hard to assess with density alone, or who should not be exposed to repeat radiation.

  • Perimenopausal and menopausal women
  • Pregnant and breastfeeding patients
  • Patients on GLP-1 medications
  • Long-term steroid users
  • Patients on aromatase inhibitors
  • Rheumatoid arthritis and autoimmune patients
  • Post-fracture and high fall-risk patients
  • Men with low testosterone or on androgen deprivation
  • Cancer survivors and patients in active therapy
  • Adults 55+ in proactive prevention
  • Anyone monitoring treatment response over time

Condition-specific summaries for clinical and employer partners are available on request.
