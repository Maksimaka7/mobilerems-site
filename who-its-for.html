Across the life span

REMS® works for the people standard bone screening leaves out.

Because REMS® is radiation-free and produces both density and quality data, it is clinically appropriate for populations that historically have had limited bone assessment options.

Pregnancy and postpartum

The only bone assessment safe during pregnancy. Establishes a maternal baseline before the largest bone draw of a woman's life.

Perimenopause and menopause

Bone loss accelerates significantly during the menopause transition. REMS® catches it earlier than standard screening protocols allow.

GLP-1 medication users

Rapid weight loss is associated with measurable bone density decline. A baseline assessment protects what the scale doesn't show.

Type 2 diabetes

Bone density alone underestimates fracture risk in diabetic patients. REMS® bone quality assessment is more accurate for this population.

Cancer therapy patients

Treatment-related bone loss is significant. Radiation-free monitoring is appropriate for patients already exposed to therapeutic radiation.

Long-term medication users

Glucocorticoids, proton pump inhibitors, aromatase inhibitors, and other medications affect bone over time. REMS® allows safe repeated monitoring.

Pre-surgical planning

Pre-operative bone assessment for joint replacement and spine surgery. Helps surgical teams plan and manage post-operative outcomes.

Active aging and athletes

Performance-oriented individuals tracking bone health alongside training load. Repeated safe monitoring supports long-term planning.

Family history of osteoporosis

Establishing a baseline before peak bone loss begins. The earlier the picture is known, the more can be done about it.
